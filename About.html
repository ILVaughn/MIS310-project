What’s this site for?

This site was created because many people (once including myself) want to get into their dream job in the industry without clear guidance on where to start. It’s hard to know what to include, how to organize projects, or which programs and platforms you actually need for your field. My goal with this site is to make the portfolio-building process less intimidating by offering a step-by-step breakdown to help you develop a portfolio that’s professional, clean, and unique. Strong portfolios are crucial in graphic design, marketing, animation, film, videography and game design, especially early on in your career to land your internship or clients. This project is meant to give creatives and students a clearer starting point to help them feel more confident before they jump into the industry.
