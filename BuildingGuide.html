What's a Portfolio?

If you're new to a creative field or still in school, you might be wondering what actually belongs in a portfolio. The good news is that you don't need years of experience or dozens of projects to get started. A portfolio is simply a collection of your work that tangibly highlights your skills, interests, and strengths as a creative. In industries like graphic design, animation, brand marketing, film, videography, and UX/UI, portfolios often matter just as much as the resume. Schools, employers, and clients use them to see the quality of your work, your specific style, and whether you're a good fit for an opportunity in schooling, internships, freelance work, or other roles.

So, let's break down the process of building a portfolio!
